INSTITUTE MOBILITY SIMULATION

Bean Run

Press Space or tap the field to deploy Bean. Arrow keys jump, duck, and discharge acquired lightning.

MOVEMENT PROTOCOL

Two threats, two responses

Space or ↑ jumps armed ground squirrels. Hold ↓ to duck airborne squirrels. Touch controls use the same physics.

ACQUIRED CAPABILITY

Lightning is tactical

Collect a field charge, then press → or Strike. The charge is only consumed when a hostile target is in range.

ESCALATION MODEL

Five explicit threat levels

Speed, hostile type, and obstacle spacing change in controlled tiers. The simulation never creates a separate mobile ruleset.
