INSTITUTE DEFENSE SIMULATION

Bean Galaga

Arrow keys / A-D to move left and right. Space to fire. P or Escape pauses.

FIRE CONTROL

Two-shot discipline

Single Bean can only keep two missiles on screen. Dual Bean fires parallel missiles with a four-projectile cap; hold FIRE for cabinet-style rapid fire.

ARCADE MOVEMENT

Horizontal fighter rail

Like the 1981 cabinet, Bean moves laterally along the bottom of the playfield. There is intentionally no vertical fighter movement.

BOSS CONTROL

Bomb first, beam later

Bosses begin with normal bombing runs. Tractor-beam attempts are delayed until mid-battle, use a fixed attack lane, and provide a visible dodge window.
